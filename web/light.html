A 100 MW data center turns almost all of its electricity into heat. Alpine decides, every hour, where that heat goes: sold to buildings nearby, stored in a hot-water pit for later, or thrown away by dry coolers (no water) or cooling towers (which evaporate water). Press Play to watch a year at the real site in Lansing, NY.
